Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Files you choose

AK Premium does not collect, store or transmit the files you choose. Built-in tools process files locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Library files load from public CDNs, but your PDF, image and spreadsheet files are not uploaded to an AK Premium server.

Accounts and personal data

AK Premium does not require an account, and does not ask you to sign up to use the built-in tools. If you contact us by email at nomanalicgr@gmail.com, we will only use your message to reply to you.

Cookies and consent

AK Premium shows a cookie consent banner. Advertising and analytics scripts are loaded only after you accept. If you reject, those scripts are not loaded and no advertising or analytics cookies are set by this site. You can change your choice by clearing your browser storage for this site.

Google AdSense and third-party vendors

AK Premium may show ads from Google AdSense once an AdSense account is approved and a client ID is set. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices. Ad slots on this site stay empty and invisible until an AdSense client ID is set, and no ad is placed inside or touching a tool interaction area or a button.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics is loaded only after you accept cookies and only when a measurement ID is set. It collects anonymous usage information, such as which pages are visited, to help improve the site. No advertising or analytics runs while the measurement ID is empty.

Third-party libraries

The site loads open libraries such as pdf-lib, pdf.js, jsPDF, SheetJS, Tesseract, mammoth, docx, pptxgenjs, JSZip and QRCode from public CDNs so tools can run in your browser. Those CDNs may log standard technical information, such as your IP address, when they serve a library file, as any web server does. The qpdf WebAssembly build used for Protect PDF and Unlock PDF is self-hosted on this site, not loaded from a CDN.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at nomanalicgr@gmail.com.
