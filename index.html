Frequently Asked Questions

What is AK Premium?

AK Premium is a free, browser-based toolkit with 117+ tools for PDF, image, text, Excel, everyday, developer, calculator, business, design and Pakistan tasks. Every tool runs in your browser, with no sign-up.

Is AK Premium free?

Yes. The built-in tools on AK Premium are free to use in your browser.

Do files leave my browser?

No. Built-in AK Premium tools process files locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Files are not uploaded to an AK Premium server.

Can I preview a file before converting it?

Yes. When you choose a PDF, the tool page shows its name, size and page count and renders page 1 as a preview under the file chooser before you convert, so you can confirm it is the right document. Images show a thumbnail. Previews are made in your browser and files are not uploaded.

What are the limits of AK Premium?

Each tool page states its own limits honestly. For example, PDF compression only re-encodes the photos inside a file, so a text-only PDF shrinks little, and Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF are basic text conversions that do not preserve exact layout, fonts or images. Scanned PDFs without a text layer need OCR first.

How do I find a tool?

Use the search box at the top of the homepage, browse the category sections, or open a category hub such as PDF Tools or Calculators. Each tool has its own page, so you can bookmark it or share its link.

What country tools are there?

Pakistan has a full country pack: salary tax, Zakat, land, amounts in words, Urdu word counting and merit. Tools for India, UAE & Saudi Arabia, the UK and the US are planned for later waves.
