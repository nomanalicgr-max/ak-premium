What Image to Text does

Reading text from an image is for typed notes, a screenshot of a message, a photo of a notice board, or a scanned page that needs to become editable text. It saves retyping the whole thing by hand.

How to use Image to Text

  1. Choose one or more images.
  2. Set the quality, format or size you want.
  3. Press the main button and download the result, or the ZIP file if there is more than one.

Tips for a good result

Choose the right language before you start. Urdu text needs the Urdu option, and a clear, straight photo with good light gives far better results than a tilted or blurry one.

Limits to know about

Text recognition works best on clear printed text. Handwriting, fancy fonts, low light and tilted photos give poor results. The first run downloads language data, so it takes longer the first time.

Example

A photo of a shop notice in Urdu becomes editable Urdu text you can paste into a document.

Frequently asked questions

Is Image to Text free?

Yes. Image to Text on AK Premium is free to use in your browser, with no sign-up and no account.

Do my files leave my browser when I use Image to Text?

No. AK Premium tools run in your browser, so the files you choose are not uploaded to an AK Premium server. The libraries the tool needs load from public CDNs, but your files stay on your device.

How do I use Image to Text?

Open the tool on this page, follow the steps under How to use, and download or copy your result. The steps are written for this tool, not for a general PDF editor.

What are the limits of Image to Text?

Text recognition works best on clear printed text. Handwriting, fancy fonts, low light and tilted photos give poor results. The first run downloads language data, so it takes longer the first time.

Does it read Urdu text?

Yes. Choose Urdu (urd) in the language list before you press Read Text. English, Arabic and Hindi are also available.

Why is some text wrong or missing?

The photo may be tilted, blurry or low contrast. Try a straighter, brighter photo, and crop out anything that is not text before you upload it.
