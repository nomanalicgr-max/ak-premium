Frequently Asked Questions

What is AK Premium?

AK Premium is a free, browser-based toolkit for PDF, image, Excel, everyday, developer, calculator, business, design and Pakistan tasks. Every tool runs in your browser, with no sign-up.

Is AK Premium free?

Yes. The built-in tools on AK Premium are free to use in your browser.

Do files leave my browser?

No. Built-in AK Premium tools process files locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Files are not uploaded to an AK Premium server.

What are the limits of AK Premium?

Each tool page states its own limits honestly. For example, Strong PDF compression turns pages into images so text is no longer selectable, and Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF are basic text conversions that do not preserve exact layout, fonts or images. Scanned PDFs without a text layer need OCR first.
