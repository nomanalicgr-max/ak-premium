Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

Files you choose

AK Premium does not collect, store or transmit the files you choose. Built-in tools process files locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Library files load from public CDNs, but your PDF, image and spreadsheet files are not uploaded to an AK Premium server.

Accounts and personal data

AK Premium does not require an account, and does not ask you to sign up to use the built-in tools. If you contact us by email, we will only use your message to reply to you.

Cookies and analytics

AK Premium itself does not currently run Google Analytics, because no measurement ID has been set. If analytics or advertising is added later, that provider may set its own cookies, and its own privacy policy will apply to those cookies. This page will be updated if that changes.

Third-party libraries

The site loads open libraries such as pdf-lib, pdf.js, jsPDF and SheetJS from public CDNs so tools can run in your browser. Those CDNs may log standard technical information, such as your IP address, when they serve a library file, as any web server does.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at nomanalicgr@gmail.com.
