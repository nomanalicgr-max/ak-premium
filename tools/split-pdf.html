What Split PDF does

Splitting is for the opposite job: taking only the pages you need out of a long PDF. A student extracts chapters 2 and 3 from a 200-page book, a manager pulls one contract clause out of a full agreement, and a shop owner separates one month's invoices from a yearly file.

How to use Split PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file using the file button above.
  2. Set any options the tool shows, such as a page range, a password or a quality level.
  3. Press the main button and download or copy your result. Your file stays in your browser.

Tips for a good result

Use ranges like 1-3, 5, 8-10 to take several separate parts in one go. The tool keeps the pages in the order you list them.

Limits to know about

Split pages keep their original content and formatting. The tool cannot split a password-protected PDF, and it does not rebuild text from a scanned page.

Example

Splitting pages 1-3, 5 from a 10-page PDF gives a new PDF with exactly those 4 pages.

Your privacy

Your files are processed in your browser by Split PDF. They are not uploaded to an AK Premium server, and you do not need an account to use the tool. The libraries the tool needs load from public CDNs, and those CDNs may log standard technical information, such as your IP address, when they serve a library file. If you later accept cookies, Google AdSense and Google Analytics may set their own cookies, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Is Split PDF free?

Yes. Split PDF on AK Premium is free to use in your browser, with no sign-up and no account.

Do my files leave my browser when I use Split PDF?

No. AK Premium tools run in your browser, so the files you choose are not uploaded to an AK Premium server. The libraries the tool needs load from public CDNs, but your files stay on your device.

How do I use Split PDF?

Open the tool on this page, follow the steps under How to use, and download or copy your result. The steps are written for this tool, not for a general PDF editor.

What are the limits of Split PDF?

Split pages keep their original content and formatting. The tool cannot split a password-protected PDF, and it does not rebuild text from a scanned page.

Can I split a PDF into single pages?

Yes. Choose Extract every page and the tool downloads a ZIP file with one PDF per page, named page-1.pdf, page-2.pdf and so on.

What happens if I enter a page that does not exist?

The tool checks your ranges against the real page count first and tells you which range is outside it, so nothing is downloaded by mistake.
