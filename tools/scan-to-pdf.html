How to use Scan to PDF

  1. Open the Scan to PDF tool using the button above.
  2. Take a photo or choose an image, then save it as a PDF.
  3. Download or copy your result. Your files stay in your browser and are not uploaded to an AK Premium server.

Frequently asked questions

Is Scan to PDF free?

Yes. Scan to PDF on AK Premium is free to use in your browser, with no sign-up.

Are my files uploaded when I use Scan to PDF?

No. AK Premium tools run in your browser, so the files you choose are not uploaded to an AK Premium server.
