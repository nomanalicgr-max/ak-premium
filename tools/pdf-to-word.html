What PDF to Word does

Converting a PDF to Word is for editing a document that only exists as a PDF: a letter, a report or a form that needs changes. The Word file can then be edited, spell-checked and printed like any other document.

How to use PDF to Word

  1. Choose the file to convert using the file button above.
  2. Check the options the tool shows, such as OCR for a scanned PDF.
  3. Press Convert and download your new file.

Tips for a good result

If the PDF is a scan, tick the OCR option before converting. Otherwise the tool will find no text to convert and will tell you so.

Limits to know about

Paragraphs are rebuilt from the text positions in the PDF, so headings, plain paragraphs and simple tables usually survive. Original fonts, exact spacing, images, charts and complex tables are not preserved. This is a text reconstruction, not a pixel-perfect copy.

Example

A 2-page letter becomes a Word document with the same paragraphs in the same order, ready to edit.

Frequently asked questions

Is PDF to Word free?

Yes. PDF to Word on AK Premium is free to use in your browser, with no sign-up and no account.

Do my files leave my browser when I use PDF to Word?

No. AK Premium tools run in your browser, so the files you choose are not uploaded to an AK Premium server. The libraries the tool needs load from public CDNs, but your files stay on your device.

How do I use PDF to Word?

Open the tool on this page, follow the steps under How to use, and download or copy your result. The steps are written for this tool, not for a general PDF editor.

What are the limits of PDF to Word?

Paragraphs are rebuilt from the text positions in the PDF, so headings, plain paragraphs and simple tables usually survive. Original fonts, exact spacing, images, charts and complex tables are not preserved. This is a text reconstruction, not a pixel-perfect copy.

Will my Word file look exactly like the PDF?

No. It keeps the text and paragraph order, and short first lines are treated as headings, but fonts, spacing and images are not carried over. For exact appearance, keep the PDF.

What if my PDF is a scanned image?

Tick the OCR option. The tool reads the scanned pages with text recognition first, then builds the Word file from that text. Handwriting and blurry scans give poor results.
