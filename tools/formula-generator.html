What Formula Generator does

An Excel formula generator is for the everyday spreadsheet jobs that are easy to describe but hard to write from memory: adding up sales for one city, counting approved orders, or looking up a price from another sheet. It turns a plain description into a formula you can paste.

How to use Formula Generator

  1. Pick the task from the list, then fill in the labelled fields with your own ranges and values.
  2. Press Generate.
  3. Copy the result and paste it into Excel, then test it on a copy of your data.

Tips for a good result

Pick the task first, then fill in the labelled fields with your own ranges. The formula is built from exactly what you typed, so double-check the ranges before you paste it into Excel.

Limits to know about

The tool builds formulas from fixed templates, not from AI. It does not read your workbook and cannot guess your cell addresses. Always test a formula on a copy of your data first.

Example

Choosing SUMIF, entering B2:B20 as the range to add, A2:A20 as the range to check, and Lahore as the condition gives =SUMIF(A2:A20, "Lahore", B2:B20).
