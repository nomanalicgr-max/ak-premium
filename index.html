Frequently Asked Questions — AK Premium

What is AK Premium?

AK Premium is a free, browser-based toolkit with a PDF Studio and an Excel Automation Engine. It helps you create, merge, split, rotate and convert PDFs, and generate Excel formulas, VBA macros, regex patterns and DAX measures.

Is AK Premium free?

Yes. The built-in tools in AK Premium are free to use in your browser, with no paid API required.

Do files leave my browser?

No. Built-in AK Premium tools process files locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Files are not uploaded to a server by AK Premium.

How do I generate an Excel formula?

Open the Excel Engine, choose Formula Generator, describe what you want in plain words — for example sum with a condition — and AK Premium returns a template formula you can adapt with your own cell ranges.

How do I merge PDFs?

Open PDF Studio, choose Merge PDFs, select two or more PDF files in the order you want, and download the combined PDF. All merging happens in your browser.
