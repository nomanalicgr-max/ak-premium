What Zakat Calculator does

A Zakat calculator is for working out the Zakat due on cash, gold and silver held for one lunar year. It is used in Ramadan and at the end of a lunar year, when a person checks whether their wealth has reached the nisab.

How to use Zakat Calculator

  1. Enter your numbers in the labelled fields.
  2. Press Calculate or Convert.
  3. Read the result, which is shown on the page.

Tips for a good result

Enter the current rate per tola for gold and silver, because the nisab depends on today's prices. The calculator uses the silver nisab (52.5 tola) when you give a silver rate.

Limits to know about

This is an estimate only, not religious advice. The nisab is 7.5 tola gold or 52.5 tola silver, and Zakat is 2.5% of net zakatable wealth. The government bank-deduction nisab for Ramadan 2026 was reported as Rs 503,529, but that figure was not directly verified from an official source, so it is shown only as reported.

Example

Cash and gold worth Rs 1,000,000 above the nisab gives Rs 25,000 Zakat at 2.5%.

What is the nisab?

The nisab is the minimum wealth above which Zakat is due: 7.5 tola of gold or 52.5 tola of silver. If your wealth is below it, no Zakat is due.

How much Zakat do I pay?

2.5% of your net zakatable wealth, after subtracting debts, once it has been held for one lunar year.
