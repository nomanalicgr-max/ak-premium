Redact PDF

True redaction. Pages that contain the text are rebuilt as images with a black box burned in, so the covered text is gone and cannot be selected or copied. Pages without a match are copied unchanged.

What Redact PDF does

Redaction is for removing sensitive details before a document is shared: a CNIC number, a phone number, a bank account number or a name in a report. It is used when a document must be shared but a few details in it must not be seen by anyone.

How to use Redact PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file using the file button above.
  2. Set any options the tool shows, such as a page range, a password or a quality level.
  3. Press the main button and download or copy your result. Your file stays in your browser.

Tips for a good result

Type the exact text to redact, including spaces. Check the downloaded PDF before you share it, and open it in a PDF reader to confirm the covered text cannot be selected or copied.

Limits to know about

Redaction works on the text layer of a PDF. Pages that contain a match are rebuilt as images with the black box burned in, so the covered text is gone from those pages. Pages without a match are copied unchanged. A scanned PDF has no text layer, so redact it after OCR, not before.

Example

Redacting 35202-1234567-1 from a form covers every place that CNIC appears with a black box.

Frequently asked questions

Is Redact PDF free?

Yes. Redact PDF on AK Premium is free to use in your browser, with no sign-up and no account.

Do my files leave my browser when I use Redact PDF?

No. AK Premium tools run in your browser, so the files you choose are not uploaded to an AK Premium server. The libraries the tool needs load from public CDNs, but your files stay on your device.

How do I use Redact PDF?

Open the tool on this page, follow the steps under How to use, and download or copy your result. The steps are written for this tool, not for a general PDF editor.

What are the limits of Redact PDF?

Redaction works on the text layer of a PDF. Pages that contain a match are rebuilt as images with the black box burned in, so the covered text is gone from those pages. Pages without a match are copied unchanged. A scanned PDF has no text layer, so redact it after OCR, not before.

Is the redacted text really gone, or just covered?

It is really gone from the affected pages. Those pages are rebuilt as images with the box burned into the picture, and the original text layer on those pages is destroyed, so the text cannot be selected or copied out.

Can I redact a scanned PDF?

Not directly. A scanned PDF has no text layer to search. Run OCR PDF first to make a text PDF, then redact that copy.
