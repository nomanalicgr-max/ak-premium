What Percentage & Merit Calculator does

A marks and merit calculator is for students applying to college or university in Pakistan, where admission depends on a weighted aggregate: Matric marks, FSc marks and an entry test, each with its own weight. It is also used for any percentage or weighted score.

How to use Percentage & Merit Calculator

  1. Enter your numbers in the labelled fields.
  2. Press Calculate or Convert.
  3. Read the result, which is shown on the page.

Tips for a good result

Enter the obtained and total marks for each part, and the weight for that part. The weights must add up to 100%. The tool shows each part's contribution and the final aggregate.

Limits to know about

The tool uses a generic weighted formula: sum of (obtained / total x 100 x weight / 100). It does not know any university's own formula, so check the official merit formula for the place you are applying to.

Example

Matric 900/1100 at 10% weight, FSc 950/1100 at 40% weight, and an entry test 150/200 at 50% weight gives an aggregate of about 80.2%.

What is a merit aggregate?

A merit aggregate combines several scores into one percentage, giving each part a weight. A part with 50% weight matters five times as much as a part with 10% weight.
