About AK Premium

AK Premium is a free, browser-based toolkit for PDF, image, Excel, business, calculator, design and Pakistan tasks. It is run by Mirza Noman Ali, a data analyst from Lahore, Pakistan, who built the site because the everyday tools he needed — merging a PDF, compressing a photo, building an Excel formula, checking salary tax — were either paid, required an account, or sent his files to a server.

Why this site exists

The idea is simple: a tool should do one job well, in your browser, without asking for your files, your email or your money. AK Premium does not have paid features. Every tool on the site is free, and there is no account to create.

How the tools work

Every tool runs in your browser with client-side JavaScript. When you choose a file, it is read by your browser, processed by your browser, and the result is downloaded to your device. AK Premium does not have a server that receives your files, and it does not store them.

Honest limits

Each tool page states what its tool can and cannot do. Some conversions are basic text conversions, and some tools, such as Strong PDF compression, trade one thing for another. Those limits are stated on the tool page, not hidden.

Contact

If a tool does not work, or you have a suggestion, email nomanalicgr@gmail.com. There is no support team; the site is run by one person, and every message is read by him.
