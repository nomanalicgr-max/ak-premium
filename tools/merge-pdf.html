Merge PDF

Combine PDFs into one file.

Merge PDF

Files up to 50 MB each. Drag the thumbnails to change the order.

What Merge PDF does

People merge PDFs when a report, invoice set or application form is split across several files and needs to be sent as one document. A teacher merges chapter PDFs into one handout, a shop owner merges daily bills into one monthly file, and a student merges assignment pages before uploading them to a portal.

How to use Merge PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file using the file button above.
  2. Set any options the tool shows, such as a page range, a password or a quality level.
  3. Press the main button and download or copy your result. Your file stays in your browser.

Tips for a good result

Check the file order in the thumbnail list before you merge. Drag a file up or down if it is in the wrong place, because the merged PDF keeps exactly the order you see.

Limits to know about

Merged PDFs keep the original pages as they are. The tool does not flatten forms, remove passwords from a protected file, or fix a damaged PDF. A protected PDF must be unlocked first.

Example

Merging a 3-page invoice, a 2-page delivery note and a 1-page cover letter gives one 6-page PDF in that order.

Your privacy

Your files are processed in your browser by Merge PDF. They are not uploaded to an AK Premium server, and you do not need an account to use the tool. The libraries the tool needs load from public CDNs, and those CDNs may log standard technical information, such as your IP address, when they serve a library file. If you later accept cookies, Google AdSense and Google Analytics may set their own cookies, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Is Merge PDF free?

Yes. Merge PDF on AK Premium is free to use in your browser, with no sign-up and no account.

Do my files leave my browser when I use Merge PDF?

No. AK Premium tools run in your browser, so the files you choose are not uploaded to an AK Premium server. The libraries the tool needs load from public CDNs, but your files stay on your device.

How do I use Merge PDF?

Open the tool on this page, follow the steps under How to use, and download or copy your result. The steps are written for this tool, not for a general PDF editor.

What are the limits of Merge PDF?

Merged PDFs keep the original pages as they are. The tool does not flatten forms, remove passwords from a protected file, or fix a damaged PDF. A protected PDF must be unlocked first.

Can I change the order of the PDFs before merging?

Yes. After you choose the files, each one shows a thumbnail of its first page. Drag the thumbnails into the order you want, then press Merge PDFs.

Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can merge?

You can merge as many as your browser can hold in memory. Very large files over 50 MB per file are refused with a friendly message, because the browser would run out of memory.
