About AK Premium

AK Premium is a free toolkit that runs entirely in your browser. It helps you work with PDFs, images, spreadsheets and everyday documents without installing software and without creating an account.

What you can do here

  • PDF tools: merge, split, compress, rotate, organise, unlock, protect, crop, redact, compare, convert and more.
  • Convert tools: PDF to Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF, plus text-based conversions.
  • Excel tools: formula generator and explainer, VBA, regex and DAX helpers, and CSV conversions.
  • Image, everyday, developer, calculator, business and design tools, including invoices, resumes, QR codes and EMI and CGPA calculators.

How it works

When you choose a file, it is processed on your own device with client-side JavaScript. AK Premium does not upload your file to its own server. A preview is shown under the file chooser before you convert, so you can confirm you picked the right document.

Honest limits

Some conversions are text-based and do not preserve complex layout, fonts, tables or images. Protect PDF is not a standard PDF password: it encrypts the whole file in your browser, and only this site's Unlock tool with the same password can reopen that file. The e-Sign tool produces a visual signature image only, not a legal digital signature. Always keep an original copy of important files and test generated formulas and code on a copy of your data first.
