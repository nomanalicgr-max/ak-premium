Image Compressor

What Image Compressor does

Compressing images is for photos that are too large to upload, email or post: a product photo for an online store, a scanned document photo, or a picture for a website. Smaller images load faster and use less data.

How to use Image Compressor

  1. Choose one or more images.
  2. Set the quality, format or size you want.
  3. Press the main button and download the result, or the ZIP file if there is more than one.

Tips for a good result

If you need a file under a specific size, enter the target size in KB and let the tool lower the quality step by step until it fits. WebP usually gives the smallest file at good quality.

Limits to know about

JPEG output flattens transparency, so a PNG with a transparent background becomes a white background. PNG output keeps transparency but may stay large. The tool works on one image at a time in the browser, so very large batches take a while.

Example

A 4 MB phone photo at 80% JPEG quality often becomes about 800 KB with no visible difference on a screen.

Frequently asked questions

Is Image Compressor free?

Yes. Image Compressor on AK Premium is free to use in your browser, with no sign-up and no account.

Do my files leave my browser when I use Image Compressor?

No. AK Premium tools run in your browser, so the files you choose are not uploaded to an AK Premium server. The libraries the tool needs load from public CDNs, but your files stay on your device.

How do I use Image Compressor?

Open the tool on this page, follow the steps under How to use, and download or copy your result. The steps are written for this tool, not for a general PDF editor.

What are the limits of Image Compressor?

JPEG output flattens transparency, so a PNG with a transparent background becomes a white background. PNG output keeps transparency but may stay large. The tool works on one image at a time in the browser, so very large batches take a while.

Which format should I choose?

JPEG for photos, PNG when you need transparency or sharp text, WebP when you want the smallest file and the place you are uploading it accepts WebP.

Can I compress many images at once?

Yes. Choose several images and they are all compressed. If there is more than one, you also get a ZIP file with all of them in it.
