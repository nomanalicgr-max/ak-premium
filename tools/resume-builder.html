Resume Builder

What Resume Builder does

A resume builder is for a job seeker who needs a clean, one-page resume quickly: name, job title, contact details, skills and experience, as a PDF that can be attached to an application.

How to use Resume Builder

  1. Fill in the fields for your business, client or resume.
  2. Choose a template if the tool offers one.
  3. Press Download and save the PDF.

Tips for a good result

Keep the experience short and specific, with numbers where you can: how many records, how many reports, how much time saved. Choose a template before you download.

Limits to know about

The resume is a simple formatted PDF, not a Word template. It does not store your resume, and it does not write the content for you.

Example

A data analyst resume with a name, title, email, phone, skills and two jobs becomes a clean PDF in one click.

Your privacy

Your files are processed in your browser by Resume Builder. They are not uploaded to an AK Premium server, and you do not need an account to use the tool. The libraries the tool needs load from public CDNs, and those CDNs may log standard technical information, such as your IP address, when they serve a library file. If you later accept cookies, Google AdSense and Google Analytics may set their own cookies, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Is Resume Builder free?

Yes. Resume Builder on AK Premium is free to use in your browser, with no sign-up and no account.

Do my files leave my browser when I use Resume Builder?

No. AK Premium tools run in your browser, so the files you choose are not uploaded to an AK Premium server. The libraries the tool needs load from public CDNs, but your files stay on your device.

How do I use Resume Builder?

Open the tool on this page, follow the steps under How to use, and download or copy your result. The steps are written for this tool, not for a general PDF editor.

What are the limits of Resume Builder?

The resume is a simple formatted PDF, not a Word template. It does not store your resume, and it does not write the content for you.

Are there different resume templates?

Yes, two. Template 1 is a classic single-column resume. Template 2 has a coloured header and a different skills layout.

Can I edit the resume after downloading it?

The PDF is not editable. If you need changes, edit the fields on this page and download it again.
