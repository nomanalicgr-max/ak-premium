What Land Converter does

A land converter is for the units used in Pakistan property deals: Marla, Kanal, Acre, square feet, square yards and square metres. A buyer, a seller, a patwari or a property dealer uses it to check that a 10 Marla plot and a 1 Kanal plot are being compared fairly.

How to use Land Converter

  1. Enter your numbers in the labelled fields.
  2. Press Calculate or Convert.
  3. Read the result, which is shown on the page.

Tips for a good result

Enter the area in the unit you have, and the tool shows all the other units at once. 1 Kanal is 20 Marla, and 1 Acre is 8 Kanal.

Limits to know about

The tool converts area only. It does not give a price, a location, or legal advice about a plot, and it uses the standard Punjab unit sizes.

Example

1 Kanal is 20 Marla, 5,445 square feet and 605 square yards.
