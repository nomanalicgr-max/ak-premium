What Pakistan Salary Tax Calculator does

A Pakistan salary tax calculator is for checking how much income tax is taken from a monthly salary, and what the take-home pay is. It is used before accepting a job offer, at the start of a tax year, and when a salary changes.

How to use Pakistan Salary Tax Calculator

  1. Enter your numbers in the labelled fields.
  2. Press Calculate or Convert.
  3. Read the result, which is shown on the page.

Tips for a good result

Enter your gross monthly salary and pick the tax year. The current year is Tax Year 2027 (FY 2026-27). The calculator shows the annual tax, the monthly tax and the take-home pay.

Limits to know about

This is an estimate only, using FBR slabs for salaried individuals. It does not include rebates, allowances, other income, or tax already deducted. It is not tax advice. Tax Year 2025 is not included because its slabs were not directly verified from an official FBR document.

Example

A monthly salary of Rs 200,000 is Rs 2,400,000 a year. In Tax Year 2027 the tax is Rs 156,000 a year, about Rs 13,000 a month.
