What Amount to Words does

Amount to words is for invoices, cheques and receipts, where an amount must be written out in words as well as figures. A shop, a school and a freelancer all use it so the amount cannot be changed by adding a digit.

How to use Amount to Words

  1. Enter your numbers in the labelled fields.
  2. Press Calculate or Convert.
  3. Read the result, which is shown on the page.

Tips for a good result

Type the amount with or without commas. The tool shows the amount in English and in Urdu, with the Urdu line reading right to left.

Limits to know about

The tool handles amounts from 0 to 999,999,999. It writes rupees and paisa only, and it does not handle other currencies.

Example

125,000 becomes one hundred twenty-five thousand rupees only, and ایک لاکھ پچیس ہزار روپے صرف.

The tool handles amounts from 0 to 999,999,999. It writes rupees and paisa only, and it does not handle other currencies.

Why do I need words on a cheque?

Words are harder to change than figures. A cheque with the amount in words is harder to alter, which is why banks and businesses ask for both.

Does it work for paisa too?

Yes. An amount such as 1,250.50 becomes one thousand two hundred fifty rupees and fifty paisa only.
