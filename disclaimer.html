Documents and conversions

PDF to Word, PDF to PowerPoint and similar conversions are text-based and may not preserve original layout, fonts, tables, images, charts or animations. Scanned PDFs without a text layer cannot be read by text extraction.

Signatures and security

The e-Sign tool produces a visual signature image only. It is not a certificate-based digital signature and may not be accepted as a legal signature. Protect PDF is not a standard PDF password, as explained in the FAQ.

Generated code and formulas

Generated Excel formulas, VBA, regex and DAX are templates. Replace placeholders with your own ranges, sheet names and columns, and test them on a copy of your data before production use.
