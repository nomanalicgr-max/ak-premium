What Compress PDF does

A PDF that is too large to email or upload is the usual reason to compress one. Admission forms, job applications and WhatsApp forwards often have a file-size limit, and a compressed copy is what gets through. Compressing also helps when a PDF is stored on a phone with little free space.

How to use Compress PDF

  1. Choose your PDF file using the file button above.
  2. Set any options the tool shows, such as a page range, a password or a quality level.
  3. Press the main button and download or copy your result. Your file stays in your browser.

Tips for a good result

Start with Light if the PDF is mostly text. Use Medium for a PDF with photos, and Strong only when size matters more than being able to select or search the text.

Limits to know about

Light re-saves the file and only helps a little on image-heavy PDFs. Medium and Strong turn pages into images, so text on those pages is no longer selectable or searchable. Compression cannot fix a damaged PDF.

Example

A 12 MB scanned PDF with photos often drops to about 4 MB on Medium and under 2 MB on Strong, depending on the images.

Frequently asked questions

Is Compress PDF free?

Yes. Compress PDF on AK Premium is free to use in your browser, with no sign-up and no account.

Do my files leave my browser when I use Compress PDF?

No. AK Premium tools run in your browser, so the files you choose are not uploaded to an AK Premium server. The libraries the tool needs load from public CDNs, but your files stay on your device.

How do I use Compress PDF?

Open the tool on this page, follow the steps under How to use, and download or copy your result. The steps are written for this tool, not for a general PDF editor.

What are the limits of Compress PDF?

Light re-saves the file and only helps a little on image-heavy PDFs. Medium and Strong turn pages into images, so text on those pages is no longer selectable or searchable. Compression cannot fix a damaged PDF.

Why is my compressed PDF still large?

Image-heavy PDFs compress by re-encoding the images. If the images are already small or the PDF is mostly vector graphics, there is little left to save, and Light may only remove a few kilobytes.

Does Strong compression change my text?

Yes. Strong renders each page as an image, so the text becomes part of the picture. You can still read it, but you cannot select, copy or search it. Use Light or Medium if you need selectable text.
