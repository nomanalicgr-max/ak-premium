CSV to Excel

What CSV to Excel does

Use CSV to Excel when you need to cSV text saved as .xlsx. It is made for that one job, and it does it in your browser, so you do not have to install anything or create an account. People use it at work, at home and at school, whenever the job comes up and a quick, free tool is what they need.

How to use CSV to Excel

  1. Pick the task from the list, then fill in the labelled fields with your own ranges and values.
  2. Press Generate.
  3. Copy the result and paste it into Excel, then test it on a copy of your data.

Tips for a good result

Start with a small file or a short piece of text the first time, so you can see how the result looks. Once you are happy with it, use the tool on your real file.

Limits to know about

This tool works in your browser, so it is limited by what a browser can do. Very large files may take a while, and the result is a browser-made file, not a desktop program's output. Check the result before you rely on it.

Example

Try it once with a sample file and you will see the result in under a minute.
