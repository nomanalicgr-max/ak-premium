Nothing on AK Premium is professional advice. Tax and Zakat calculators are estimates only, based on the figures stated on their pages. They are not tax advice, legal advice or religious advice. Consult a qualified professional before you act on a result.

Conversion limits

PDF to Word, PDF to Excel and PDF to PPT rebuild text from the PDF text layer, so layout, fonts, images and complex tables are not preserved. Word, Excel and PowerPoint to PDF are basic text conversions with the same limits. Scanned PDFs without a text layer need OCR first, and OCR is not perfect.

e-Sign is visual only

The e-Sign tool produces a visual signature image. It is not a legal digital signature and does not prove who signed a document.

Generated code and formulas

Generated Excel formulas, VBA macros, regex patterns and DAX measures are templates built from fixed rules. They have not been run against your workbook or your data. Test them on a copy of your data before you use them.
