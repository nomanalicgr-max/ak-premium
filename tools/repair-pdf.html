How to use Repair PDF

  1. Open the Repair PDF tool using the button above.
  2. Choose your damaged PDF and let the tool try to re-save it.
  3. Download or copy your result. Your files stay in your browser and are not uploaded to an AK Premium server.

Good to know

Honest limit: repair is best effort. A badly damaged PDF may still not open after re-saving.
