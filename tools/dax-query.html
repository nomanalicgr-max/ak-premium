What DAX Query does

A DAX measure is for Power BI and Excel Power Pivot: a total, an average, a year-to-date figure, or a share of the total that updates when a user filters the report. It is the calculation behind most Power BI cards and charts.

How to use DAX Query

  1. Pick the task from the list, then fill in the labelled fields with your own ranges and values.
  2. Press Generate.
  3. Copy the result and paste it into Excel, then test it on a copy of your data.

Tips for a good result

Use your real table and column names in the fields. The measure name is the name that will appear in your report, so make it clear, such as Total Sales.

Limits to know about

The tool builds measures from fixed templates. It does not connect to your Power BI file and cannot check that your table or column names are right. Paste the measure into Power BI and test it there.

Example

Choosing Total Sales, with table Sales and column Amount, gives Total Sales = SUM(Sales[Amount]).

What is DAX?

DAX is the formula language of Power BI and Excel Power Pivot. A measure is a calculation, such as a total, that is worked out when a user looks at the report.

Why does my measure show an error in Power BI?

The table or column name is usually the reason. Check the spelling and the square brackets, and make sure the table exists in your model.
