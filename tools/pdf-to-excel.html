PDF to Excel

What PDF to Excel does

Converting a PDF to Excel is for getting numbers out of a PDF table: a bank statement, a price list, a stock report or a fee table that needs to be sorted, totalled or charted. Once the numbers are in Excel, formulas can work on them.

How to use PDF to Excel

  1. Choose the file to convert using the file button above.
  2. Check the options the tool shows, such as OCR for a scanned PDF.
  3. Press Convert and download your new file.

Tips for a good result

Tables with clear gaps between columns convert best. If a table has no gaps, the whole line lands in one cell and you can use Text to Columns in Excel to split it.

Limits to know about

Columns are detected from the gaps between words on each line. Tables with merged cells, no gaps or rotated text may not split cleanly, and numbers are stored as text in some cases, so check a few cells before you trust a total.

Example

A price list PDF with item, quantity and price columns becomes a spreadsheet with those three columns.

How do I use PDF to Excel?

Open the tool on this page, follow the steps under How to use, and download or copy your result. The steps are written for this tool, not for a general PDF editor.

What are the limits of PDF to Excel?

Columns are detected from the gaps between words on each line. Tables with merged cells, no gaps or rotated text may not split cleanly, and numbers are stored as text in some cases, so check a few cells before you trust a total.

Why did my table land in one column?

The PDF had no clear gap between its columns, so the tool kept the whole line together. In Excel, select the column, use Data, Text to Columns, and split on spaces or a delimiter.

Are the numbers in my Excel file real numbers?

They are text in most cases, because a PDF does not say whether a word is a number. In Excel, select the column and use Paste Special, Values, or multiply by 1 to turn text numbers into real numbers.
